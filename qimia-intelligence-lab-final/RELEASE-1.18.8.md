# Qimia Intelligence Lab 1.18.8

Mini-cart clarity polish only.

## What changed

- **Smart header:** the compact cashback ladder now sits flush under the mini-cart title as a full-width rectangular header with less wasted space.
- **Cart items clarity:** the shopper's actual mini-cart items are made more obvious with a stronger “In your cart” section divider and clearer card spacing.
- **Added badge:** each real mini-cart line now carries a small Added badge so it is immediately clear which items are already in the cart.
- **Readability:** product names reserve space for the badge, and the quantity/price line is more visible.

## Unchanged

- WooCommerce add-to-cart, mini-cart refresh, totals and checkout logic.
- Cashback logic, thresholds, rewards and messages.
- Product prices, stock and discount logic.
- Recommendation helper panel, cart-page ladder placement, Flash Sale, My Qimia, search, comparison and AI behaviour.
