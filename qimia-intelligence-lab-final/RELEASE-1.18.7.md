# Qimia Intelligence Lab 1.18.7

Mini-cart presentation polish only.

## What changed

- **Mini-cart ladder:** the compact cashback ladder remains the first element in the mini cart, now with tighter spacing and a more header-like presentation.
- **Real cart items:** a small divider labelled **In your cart** now appears before the shopper's actual mini-cart items, with a quiet item-count pill.
- **Visual separation:** actual mini-cart items now use a subtle white card treatment, while the recommendation area below keeps its own soft Tiffany helper panel. This makes added items and suggested items much easier to distinguish.

## Unchanged

- Cart logic, totals, add-to-cart behaviour and WooCommerce fragments.
- Cashback logic, thresholds, rewards and messages.
- Product prices, discount display and stock logic.
- Cart-page ladder placement from 1.18.6, Flash Sale, My Qimia, search, comparison and AI behaviour.
